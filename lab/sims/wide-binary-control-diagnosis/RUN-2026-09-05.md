## Verdict

No failed control implicates the Astrolabe apparatus. R0 is a defect of the frozen protocol's own uncertainty method, which the fixture implemented faithfully: 1.4826*MAD over 3-5 seeds collapses whenever a few seeds happen to cluster, and it is used as a denominator by every gate. R3 is a fixture defect -- the arms planted bound Keplerian pairs where the protocol specified pairs that are explicitly 'not physical' -- compounded by a gate that compares the estimate against a ratio built on a different denominator. R4 combines an unpaired reading of an ambiguous rule with a requirement of >=80% detection over three seeds, reachable only at 3/3; paired against the matched zero-injection arm the apparatus recovers the injection in every seed of every arm. R1 and cap isolation are undetermined: the frozen 0.04 threshold lies inside the null's own spread, so that decision layer cannot separate a result from its null.

This is a diagnosis of a synthetic apparatus calibration. It makes no
observational claim, no claim about gravity, and it does not change ORB-11222's
frozen verdict, which remains **unresolved**. Frozen terms touched:
none -- thresholds, seed matrix, shift ladder and run rows are read from the ORB-11221 blob and left as they are.

## Per-control diagnosis

### R0 sanity — frozen outcome FAIL, classified `protocol-arithmetic-defect`

Locus: the frozen protocol's own uncertainty method; Astrolabe not implicated.

Exactly one of eight R0 bins fired. Its five per-seed B_i have sample sd 0.0198 but 1.4826*MAD 0.0039 -- the MAD understates the spread 5.1x because three of five values happen to cluster. Under a true null the same gate fires on at least one of eight bins 70.3% of the time at n=5.

The estimator is the protocol's, not the fixture's: the frozen uncertainty method is 'median +/- 1.4826xMAD ... across the run's seed list', and the fixture implemented it faithfully. The protocol says a R0 failure indicts the synthetic framework rather than Astrolabe; this diagnosis agrees and narrows it further, to the frozen decision layer rather than the population.

### R1 geometry/truncation and cap isolation — frozen outcome FAIL, classified `undetermined`

Locus: decision layer cannot support a verdict either way.

The zero-injection null itself spans B_i in [-0.0945, +0.1655] with sd 0.0418; 34.2% of null seed-bins already sit at or above the frozen 0.04 threshold, so the threshold lies inside the null floor. The reproducibility clause (|median| > 1.4826*MAD) fires on a true null 44.1% of the time at n=3 and 24.3% at n=5, and the cap comparison disagrees spuriously 16.7% of the time.

Not reclassified as a pass: the frozen arms genuinely cleared the frozen threshold, and the reproducibility clause is the protocol's own, faithfully implemented. The claim is only that this decision layer cannot distinguish that result from its own null, so the arms neither support nor refute the geometry hypothesis as scored.

### R3 shifted-field calibration — frozen outcome FAIL, classified `fixture-defect + protocol-arithmetic-defect`

Locus: population generator (Orrery fixture) plus the gate's denominator; Astrolabe not implicated.

Protocol step 4 asks for planted pairs that are explicitly 'not physical pairs'; the fixture planted newtonian_mock_pairs output through the true-pair path, giving both components one shared distance and Keplerian relative PM. The genuine chance-alignment content of the selected sample is 0.0000 (R3-10) and 0.0009 (R3-20), and the estimator returned within 0.0010 of that in every arm. It was scored against contamination that was never injected, in a denominator it does not use.

Positive control: With genuine chance alignments present the estimator tracks them: label truth 0.0000 -> 0.0777 across the field-PM ladder, estimate 0.0000 -> 0.1630, monotone and shift-insensitive.

### R4 known-effect power — frozen outcome FAIL, classified `fixture-defect + protocol-arithmetic-defect`

Locus: decision layer (Orrery fixture) and the frozen rule's own arithmetic.

The apparatus recovers the injection cleanly. Differencing each injected seed against the same seed of the zero-injection arm gives 85% of the 0.05 amplitude and 83% of the 0.10 amplitude, positive in every seed of every arm. The frozen gate instead tested unpaired |B_i| against zero using a spread that carries the whole comparator-sampling budget, and its >=80% requirement is unreachable below 3/3 with the three seeds the frozen matrix allots.

The 80%-of-3-seeds inconsistency is recorded, not repaired; it sits with the protocol's 47-vs-44 realization count.

The last column is a second finding, and a negative one. The protocol names a
shift-dependent R_chance at fixed true contamination as its own miscalibration
signal, and the estimate does move with the shift: by 34% of itself even at

Both of these — the overshoot and the shift dependence — appear only in this
probe's deliberately contaminated regime, reached by cutting the field PM
dispersion by 10–30×. In the frozen R3 arms the rate is ~0 and the whole
shift-ladder spread is 0.0041 absolute, so neither affects the ORB-11222
verdict.

The estimate sits systematically above the truth, by a factor of
1.8–2.9 across the
probe. An independent sky-position scramble, which shares no code with the RA
shift beyond the selector itself, reproduces the same overshoot, so this is a
property of randomisation-based chance estimation here rather than of one code
branch.

Attempting to pin the mechanism down further was **inconclusive**. The probe
lowered the true-pair count to see whether the overshoot tracked binary content,
but that ladder confounds what it varies: fewer true pairs also frees room under
the 8,000-star brightness cap for field stars, so the chance-alignment truth
itself moves between rungs instead of staying fixed. The ratio neither falls
monotonically nor keeps the two randomisations in agreement at the low rungs. A
controlled test would hold the selected field population fixed while varying only
binary content. The magnitude is reproducible; the cause is not established
here. That is an open question for Astrolabe's own task, not diagnosed in this
repository, and it does not bear on the ORB-11222 verdict —
those arms contain essentially no genuine chance alignments for the estimator to
over- or under-count.
