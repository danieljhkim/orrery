# RAR network-force fluctuation limit

## Question and declared interpretation

If the acceleration beyond that produced by visible baryons is generated by a
dynamic, connection-forming network, how large can a galaxy-to-galaxy
fluctuation in that extra term be without broadening the observed radial
acceleration relation (RAR) beyond its 0.11-dex Gaussian width?

The observable is the standard deviation of
`log10(g_obs) - log10(g_RAR(g_bar))`. A value of `sigma_net` is excluded at 95%
when at least 95% of mock populations are broader than 0.11 dex. A network
model predicting a larger coherent fluctuation is weakened; a smaller value is
not supported by this test, merely not excluded. Non-log-normal, baryon-linked,
or time-averaged fluctuations remain unresolved.

Run the deterministic capture from the repository root:

```bash
uv run lab/sims/rar-network-fluctuation-limit/main.py
```

This refreshes `assets/results.json` and `assets/exclusion-curve.png`.

## Apparatus

The seeded mock contains 153 galaxies and exactly 2693 resolved points, matching
the quality-cut scale of the original SPARC RAR analysis. Each galaxy spans a
broad inner-to-outer baryonic-acceleration sequence. The static ground truth is

`g_RAR = g_bar / (1 - exp(-sqrt(g_bar/g_dagger)))`,

with `g_dagger = 1.20e-10 m/s^2`. The injected network term is

`g_obs = g_bar + (g_RAR - g_bar) * 10^delta`,

where `delta` is Normal(0, `sigma_net`) in dex. The default draw is fully
coherent across radii within a galaxy and independent between galaxies.

The observational forward model includes pointwise rotation-velocity error and
galaxy-correlated distance, inclination, stellar mass-to-light, and H I flux
errors. The full-scale parameters are 0.03 dex in `g_obs`, 0.08 dex in distance,
4 degrees in inclination, 0.11 dex in stellar M/L, and 0.04 dex in H I flux.
Their realized residual contributions are captured beside the 2016 scatter
budget. The declared fiducial scales these nuisance amplitudes by 0.75; the
zero-, half-, and full-budget results are retained rather than selecting an
error model after seeing the answer.

## Result

The fiducial observational model produces a median baseline scatter of 0.0866
dex. Its 95% exclusion crossing is

`sigma_net < 0.171 dex` (galaxy-coherent, fiducial 0.75 error budget).

Thus a model predicting a one-sigma coherent fluctuation larger than a factor
`10^0.171 = 1.48` in the extra acceleration is excluded by this apparatus. This
is a limit, not a detection or preference for a 48% fluctuation.

The error budget controls the answer. With the full 2016 budget, whose mock
baseline is already 0.1156 dex, the 95% limit tightens to 0.0696 dex (a factor
1.17). With no observational errors the absolute 0.11-dex-width ceiling is
0.243 dex (a factor 1.75). Half-budget errors give 0.206 dex. At the fiducial
budget, mixed radial coherence gives 0.157 dex and independent pointwise
fluctuations give 0.154 dex.

The nested 64-, 128-, and 256-realization estimates are 0.163, 0.173, and
0.171 dex. Changing the mock population from 76 to 306 galaxies moves the
bound from 0.173 to 0.154 dex; the finite-population convergence envelope is
therefore 0.154–0.173 dex. The captured JSON is authoritative for the complete
curve, percentile bands, sensitivity cases, and unrounded values.

## Limitations

- This is a synthetic SPARC-shaped population, not a hierarchical likelihood
  fit to the published catalog. Its acceleration and stellar/gas mixtures are
  plausible distributions, not galaxy-by-galaxy reconstructions.
- The historical 2016 error budget totals 0.12 dex in quadrature, slightly more
  than the fitted 0.11-dex Gaussian ridge. Any finite limit is consequently
  dominated by assumptions about correlated observational errors. The
  full-budget case is shown explicitly.
- Gaussian distance, inclination, M/L, H I, and velocity nuisances omit
  asymmetric rotation curves, non-circular motions, selection effects, and
  covariance between inferred galaxy properties.
- Multiplying only the positive extra term is one operational reading of a
  network force. Radially structured, baryon-correlated, non-log-normal, or
  rapidly time-averaged dynamics need different injections.
- The scatter is recovered about the fixed published RAR rather than refitting
  its functional form in every realization.

## Sources

- McGaugh, Lelli & Schombert (2016), *The Radial Acceleration Relation in
  Rotationally Supported Galaxies*, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.201101. This is
  the source of the 153/2693 sample scale, equation, 0.11-dex Gaussian width,
  and tabulated error budget.
- Li, Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert (2018), *Fitting the radial acceleration
  relation to individual SPARC galaxies*, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/201732547. This
  motivates treating distance, inclination, and M/L as galaxy-level nuisances;
  its fitted residual width is 0.057 dex after marginalizing them.
